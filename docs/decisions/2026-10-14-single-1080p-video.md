# One 1080p file per video

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Approved by:** Saran
**Affects:** DESIGN.md §8.2, §9; scripts/ingest; VideoPlayer

## What changed
Videos are stored as **one 1080p MP4**, not 1080p plus 720p. The file is encoded by quality rather than at a fixed bitrate: CRF 22, capped at 4 Mbps.

## Why
Saran wanted to keep R2 storage under control as more videos are added. A fixed 5 Mbps 1080p file was also heavy for viewers on mobile data.

Results on the 30-year montage (4:28):

| Option | Stored | One full viewing | Quality vs master (SSIM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1080p at 5 Mbps + 720p at 2.5 Mbps (old plan) | 241 MB | 82 MB (720p) or 159 MB (HD) | 0.989 (1080p) |
| **One 1080p, CRF 22, max 4 Mbps** | **98 MB** | **98 MB** | **0.985** |

A side-by-side crop of a detailed group photo showed no visible difference.

## Notes
- The video schema still accepts an optional `720` file, for an unusually long video. When a video has one, the player starts with the 720p copy and shows an HD link.
- Video files are never overwritten in R2. A re-edit gets a new file name.
